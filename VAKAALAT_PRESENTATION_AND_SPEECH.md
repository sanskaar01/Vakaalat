## Slide 2: The Real Problem Nobody Admits
**On the Slide:**
- **Why 95% of everyday wrongs go unreported:**
  1. *"I don't even know if a law was actually broken."*
  2. *"Walking into a lawyer's chamber feels too dramatic—and too expensive."*
  3. *"What if my name, my school, or my family gets dragged into this?"*

**Your Nonchalant Speech:**
> "Look at why ordinary people in India don't seek legal help. It's almost never because they don't care.
> First, nobody teaches us the actual *Dhaarein*—you don't know if what happened to you is a serious criminal offense under BNS or just bad luck.
> Second, going to a physical court or hunting for a lawyer in Civil Lines feels huge and intimidating.
> And third—people are terrified of exposure. Nobody wants their real name, their phone number, or how much money they lost floating around in public."

## Slide 4: The 4-Pillar Live Legal Computation Engine
**On the Slide:**
- **Write your story raw & real → Gemini computes 4 Pillars live:**
  1. **What the laws say** — Exact count of *Dhaarein*, BNS sections & Constitutional Articles violated.
  2. **What that wrongdoer deserves** — Statutory jail time, fines, compensation & disciplinary remedies.
  3. **How serious it is** — Seriousness score (`/10`) & honest check on whether it's beyond first-aid.
  4. **What you can do right now** — Official Govt Portals *(with hover `"i"` step-by-step guides)* + YouTube Knowledge Trailers.

**Your Nonchalant Speech:**
> "Here's what happens when you type your story into our desk—raw, in your own tone, English or Hinglish, or even by voice.
> In the backend, our engine runs your exact story through four strict pillars:
> Pillar 1 tells you how many *Dhaarein* the other person broke—and validates that no, you're not crazy, you were wronged.
> Pillar 2 tells you straight up what that person deserves under Indian law—jail time, fines, or suspension.
> Pillar 3 measures how serious it is out of 10—and if it's too serious for DIY digital steps, it tells you honestly to get a lawyer near you.
> And Pillar 4 is our **Digital First-Aid**. We don't just dump government links—every portal has a little **`i` button** where hovering tells you the exact 3 steps to take on that site. Plus, we link YouTube knowledge guides with a short text trailer—like if someone's private photo was leaked, we show them how government & StopNCII portals use cryptographic hashcodes to delete that image worldwide without ever re-uploading it."

  - `Blur OFF` → `₹4,20,000` / `₹69,000 (representational)`

**Your Nonchalant Speech:**
> "Privacy isn't a checkbox for us—it's baked into the UI in a slightly fun way.
> If you're a citizen on Vakaalat, you literally *cannot* upload a profile picture. Everybody gets a stage alias.
> And when you post a story involving money or IDs, our masker strips it automatically. We even put a **Blur ON / Blur OFF** button right in the top bar. When Blur is ON, amounts are blurred out. And if someone clicks Blur OFF hoping to snoop on how much money you actually lost? It swaps the numbers with funny representational figures like `₹4,20,000` or `₹69,000`. The context stays intact, the vibe stays light, and your real numbers never leak."

## Slide 9: Why Windows XP + Day Sun & Night Minecraft Sky?
**On the Slide:**
- **Psychological Design Choice:** Corporate legal sites feel cold, intimidating, and transactional. Nostalgic tactile UI makes people feel safe.
- **Day Mode (Sun):** Classic XP Royal Blue frames, swaying coconut trees, green grass & rotating sun loader.
- **Night Mode (Moon):** Pitch matte black (`#050506`), subtle gray window headers, Minecraft pixel stars, real constellations *(Libra, Orion, Cassiopeia, Big Dipper)* & moon-phase loader.

**Your Nonchalant Speech:**
> "People always ask—why the Windows XP buttons, the coconut trees in the daytime, and the pitch-black Minecraft pixel stars at night?
> Honestly? Because every other legal website looks like a terrifying government tax form or a soulless corporate SaaS template. When someone is hurt or stressed, sterile corporate UI makes them clam up.
> Giving Vakaalat tactile Windows XP windows, hand-drawn ink arrows, a sunny tropical breeze during the day, and a quiet pitch-black pixelated constellation sky with moon phases at night makes the whole space feel human, nostalgic, and disarming. You actually *want* to stay and write what happened."
