# Vakaalat — Strategic Horizons & Platform Roadmap

> *"A measured, deliberate journey from stealth prototype to national legal infrastructure."*

---

## 🗺️ The Four Horizons

```
[ HORIZON 1: GENESIS ] ──► [ HORIZON 2: MULTILINGUAL ] ──► [ HORIZON 3: ECOSYSTEM ] ──► [ HORIZON 4: NATIONAL ]
  • Core 3-tier architecture   • Voice & vernacular intake    • Bar enrollment verification  • Pan-India High Courts
  • Svelte 5 / Runes engine    • 8 Indian languages           • e-Courts case tracker sync   • NALSA & legal aid bridge
  • Delhi NCR pilot cohort     • Deep statutory indexing      • Advocate office workspace    • Sovereign privacy standard
```

---

### Horizon 1: Genesis & Controlled Pilot (Active Horizon)
**Target Focus: Delhi NCR & Uttar Pradesh Cohort**

- [x] **Architectural Blueprint & Governance Model:** Canonical documentation, finite state machines, and legal boundary enforcement.
- [x] **Brand & Sensory Foundation:** Courtroom design system, bilingual typography (Tiro Devanagari Hindi + Instrument Serif), and GSAP-driven Weighing Scale physics.
- [x] **Sovereign Intake Pipeline:** Natural language intake, provenance tracking (`USER_STATED` vs `AI_UNDERSTOOD`), and citizen fact confirmation.
- [ ] **Zero-Knowledge Privacy Redaction Engine:** Automated 3-tier PII scrubbing transforming raw narratives into Generalized Help Pleas.
- [ ] **Advocate Merit Feed & Niche Routing:** Practice-area filtering across 21 canonical categories and ~75 subcategories.
- [ ] **Consented Gateway:** Explicit citizen-granted disclosure unlocking encrypted contact details.
- [ ] **Controlled Closed Pilot:** 50 diverse citizen dispute scenarios tested against 5 practicing advocates across district and high court jurisdictions.

---

### Horizon 2: Vernacular Expansion & Decision Graph
**Target Focus: Language Inclusivity & Procedural Precision**

- [ ] **Speech-to-Text Intake:** Direct voice recording support for citizens unable to write or type, transcribing colloquial vernacular into structured chronologies.
- [ ] **Expansion to 8 Regional Languages:** Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Kannada, and Punjabi.
- [ ] **Procedural Timeline Generator:** Visual step-by-step guidance on limitation periods, statutory notice wait-times, and required filings under BNS/BNSS.
- [ ] **Emergency Gold-Hour Routing:** Automated detection of urgent statutory deadlines (e.g., 24-hour cyber fraud freezing, 30-day cheque dishonour notices).

---

### Horizon 3: Verified Bar Integration & Court Ecosystem
**Target Focus: Advocate Integrity & Workflow Efficiency**

- [ ] **Direct Bar Council Verification:** Automated verification of State Bar Council enrollment certificates and identity credentials.
- [ ] **e-Courts Case Status Linkage:** Real-time synchronization with District Court and High Court cause lists for ongoing matters.
- [ ] **Advocate Briefing Workspace:** Clean, structured case summaries that save lawyers hours of discovery time before the initial client conference.
- [ ] **Institutional Legal Aid Bridge:** Seamless handoff to NALSA, State Legal Services Authorities (SLSA), and District Legal Services Authorities (DLSA) for indigent citizens.

---

### Horizon 4: National Public Utility
**Target Focus: Sovereign Digital Public Infrastructure**

- [ ] Open API standards for legal aid non-profits and university legal clinics.
- [ ] End-to-end homomorphic privacy verification ensuring zero central storage of unencrypted citizen identities.
- [ ] Institutional adoption across all 25 High Court jurisdictions and 700+ District Courts in India.
