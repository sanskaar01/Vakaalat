## 2. Bar Council of India Compliance (Rule 36)

Rule 36 of Chapter II, Part VI of the Bar Council of India Rules states:
> *"An advocate shall not solicit work or advertise, either directly or indirectly, whether by circulars, advertisements, touts, personal communications, interviews not warranted by personal relations, furnishing or inspiring newspaper comments or producing his photographs to be published in connection with cases in which he has been engaged or concerned."*

### How Vakaalat Respects Rule 36:
1. **No Commercial Ranking:** Advocates are never ranked by spend, fees, or artificial algorithms.
2. **Merit-First Discovery:** Advocates browse sanitized pleas filtered solely by:
   - Practice Subcategory (e.g., *Cheque Bounce / Section 138 NI Act*, *Consumer Dispute*, *Tenancy & Eviction*).
   - District or High Court Jurisdiction.
3. **No Fee Bidding:** There are no price wars or reverse-auction mechanisms on the platform.
4. **Professional Dignity:** Advocate profiles present factual, verifiable credentials (Bar Council Enrollment Number, Year of Enrollment, Admitted Courts, and Primary Practice Areas).

## 3. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023

Under the DPDP Act 2023, every citizen is a *Data Principal*, and platforms processing sensitive information are *Data Fiduciaries*.

Vakaalat implements strict compliance:
- **Purpose Limitation:** Information provided during intake is processed solely for generating the structured summary and matching with qualified advocates.
- **Data Minimization:** No unnecessary demographic, financial, or tracking data is requested.
- **Consent Manager Design:** The citizen retains the right to:
  - Keep the case strictly private (self-help only).
  - Withdraw or decline any advocate expression of interest.
  - Delete case history and associated logs upon request.

## 4. The 2024–2026 Criminal Law Transitions

On July 1, 2024, the Republic of India implemented the new criminal codes:
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)** replacing the Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC).
- **Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)** replacing the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC).
- **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)** replacing the Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA).

Vakaalat incorporates canonical cross-referencing between older sections and new enactments, ensuring citizens and advocates are aligned with current procedural realities without legal ambiguity.
